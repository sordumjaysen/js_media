01 / The Challenge

Fragmented Customer Touchpoints Across Multiple Venues

Operating across three physical boutique locations, the brand enjoyed a loyal in-person following, but suffered from disconnected digital discovery. Each location maintained uncoordinated Google Business profiles, broken menu links, and inconsistent brand photography.

Visitors searching for dining reservations or private event bookings frequently encountered outdated PDFs or ended up on third-party aggregator portals charging steep 20% booking commissions.

02 / The Strategy

The 4-Stage Growth Framework

JS MEDIA applied our proprietary four-stage framework to align every digital touchpoint:

Stage 01 / Understand

Digital Audit & Journey Mapping

Mapped how guests discovered each venue, diagnosing where high-intent diners dropped off between search and booking.

Stage 02 / Plan

Channel Governance & SEO Hierarchy

Structured location-specific landing pages and established a unified content calendar highlighting seasonal chef specials.

Stage 03 / Execute

Google Maps & Direct Funnels

Claimed, verified, and enriched all 3 Google Business profiles with high-resolution imagery and direct-to-table reservation links.

Stage 04 / Review

Monthly Review & Event Uplift

Conducted monthly reviews measuring booking volume, table turns, and search impressions per location.

03 / The Execution

Direct Bookings Over Costly Intermediaries

We prioritized direct booking routes on Google Maps and Instagram bio links, routing diners directly to the venue's commission-free reservation system.

Simultaneously, we initiated an automated post-visit review sequence that boosted their verified Google reviews from 3.9★ to 4.8★ with over 320 genuine guest ratings across the three locations.

04 / The Business Impact

Autonomous Digital Growth

Within 90 days of implementation, direct online booking traffic rose by 65%, significantly reducing reliance on 3rd-party delivery and booking portals and reclaiming thousands in annual margin.

The management team now operates from a clear, repeatable quarterly marketing playbook that scales effortlessly to future venue openings.
