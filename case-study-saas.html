01 / The Challenge

Zero Organic Footprint Against Entrenched Competitors

The client provided an exceptional billing and reconciliation platform for modern enterprises, but was completely invisible on search engines. Every piece of traffic came through founder networks or expensive outbound sales reps.

When corporate finance directors searched for software comparisons, audit compliance solutions, or invoice workflow automation, legacy enterprise competitors dominated all top 10 search results.

02 / The Strategy

Bottom-of-Funnel Search Intent Over Vanity Volume

Rather than attempting to rank for ultra-broad keywords like "finance software", JS MEDIA targeted high-intent bottom-of-the-funnel commercial terms where buyers were actively seeking solutions:

Strategy 01

Comparison & Alternative Pages

Engineered objective feature comparison matrices addressing buyer research right before software selection.

Strategy 02

Technical Workflow Deep Dives

Published authoritative 2,500-word step-by-step guides on audit automation and multi-entity tax reporting.

Strategy 03

Technical SEO Foundation

Implemented JSON-LD SoftwareApplication schema markup, resolved crawl depth bottlenecks, and optimized Core Web Vitals.

03 / The Execution

Rigorous Editorial Standards & Internal Linking

We established an editorial review process with senior financial analysts to ensure every article met exacting institutional standards without fluff or generic filler.

Each guide was seamlessly interconnected through an intentional topic cluster model, funnelling search equity and reader attention straight toward book-a-demo forms.

04 / The Business Impact

Compounding Commercial Inbound

Within 4 months, organic search sessions increased by 75%, with 14 target high-intent commercial keywords advancing from unranked positions into the Top 10 Google search results.

Inbound demo requests from organic search now represent 35% of the sales team's closed-won enterprise pipeline, creating an ongoing asset that continues to deliver without incremental advertising spend.
