01 / The Challenge

Inflated Acquisition Costs & Unqualified Demo Signups

The startup had built a reliable product workflow for operations teams, but their initial self-managed Google and LinkedIn campaigns suffered from high click costs and tire-kicker leads who rarely converted to paid contracts.

Their ad spend was draining working capital with an unviable cost per demo. They needed a disciplined paid media architecture that filtered for real commercial decision-makers and minimized wasted budget on low-intent search traffic.

02 / The Strategy

Search Intent Filtering & Problem-Centric Ad Creative

We completely restructured their advertising account around high-intent decision triggers rather than generic software category keywords:

Step 01

Negative Keyword Audit

Eliminated 450+ non-commercial terms (free templates, student searches, DIY tutorials) that were bleeding 30% of daily budget.

Step 02

Pain-Point Copy Angles

Replaced technical feature jargon with direct operational pain points: wasted team hours, spreadsheet errors, and compliance overhead.

Step 03

Dedicated Landing Page Flow

Constructed a minimalist demo booking page matching the exact ad proposition, removing site navigation distractions.

03 / The Execution

Multi-Touch Retargeting & Creative Iteration

We established a tightly budgeted Meta retargeting layer. Visitors who left the landing page without booking were re-engaged with customer case snippets and 30-second UI walkthrough videos addressing common objections.

Campaigns were evaluated on weekly bidding milestones rather than vanity click-throughs, dynamically shifting spend toward the best-converting keyword ad groups.

04 / The Business Impact

Predictable Unit Economics for Series-A Readiness

Within 60 days, Cost Per Lead dropped by 24% while demo show-up rate increased from 45% to 78%.

The campaign generated over 185 qualified product demonstrations, generating a measured 2.4x return on advertising spend and providing the founders with dependable unit economics to show incoming seed investors.
