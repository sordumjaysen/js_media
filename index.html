04b // FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Clear answers on how we structure our services, contracts, and working arrangements.

Have a specific question about your project?

Each of our eight services can be engaged independently (e.g. solely SEO, paid advertising, social media management, or website design) or combined into a unified growth programme tailored to your business objectives.

We offer three clear models: Project-Based for defined one-off deliverables with set timelines (such as audits or campaign launches); Monthly Retainers for continuous management and ongoing growth; and Consultancy for high-level strategy reviews and market assessments.

We follow a four-stage framework: 01 Understand (clarify your goals, audience, and market position), 02 Plan (agree on scope, deliverables, and success metrics), 03 Execute (deliver campaigns with clear communication), and 04 Review (evaluate performance data and refine strategy).

We design, develop, and maintain professional marketing websites, e-commerce stores, and high-converting landing pages as part of our core services. However, deep custom software engineering, legacy IT infrastructure, and complex AI system development remain separate and outside our core scope.

We provide regular, transparent reporting focused on actionable commercial metrics that impact your business—such as qualified leads, customer acquisition cost, organic reach, and return on ad spend—rather than superficial vanity numbers.

Following an initial discovery discussion and mutual sign-off on the scope and deliverables, onboarding and campaign setup typically commence within 48 to 72 hours.

Yes. We offer a dedicated Customer Support Setup (WhatsApp Quick-Start) as a defined project sprint. We set up WhatsApp Business properly, configure quick replies, write a customer FAQ, build an escalation flow, and provide an operational staff guide so your internal team can manage inquiries with consistency. Daily managed responses (answering chats on your behalf) are held separately to ensure dedicated response times.
