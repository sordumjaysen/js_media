Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 • Domain: jsmedia.agency

1. Overview & Commitment

JS MEDIA ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website https://jsmedia.agency. We respect the privacy of our website visitors, prospective partners, and clients. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, and the steps we take to protect your personal data in accordance with international data protection standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information directly provided by you through our interactive inquiry builder and contact forms:

  • Contact Details: Your name and email address.
  • Commercial Briefs: Information regarding your business, selected service departments (e.g. Social Media, SEO, Advertising), engagement preferences, and project objectives.
  • Technical Logs: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic information such as browser type, operating system, and timestamp data used solely to ensure website availability and security.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly for legitimate commercial communications, including:

  • Evaluating project feasibility and preparing customized commercial proposals.
  • Transmitting the official JS MEDIA Service Catalogue upon request.
  • Communicating with you regarding active or proposed client engagements.
  • We never sell, lease, or distribute your personal information or contact details to third-party data brokers or marketing platforms.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses essential functional cookies and minimal performance telemetry to ensure fast loading times and correct interface state (such as remembering your language preference). You may decline non-essential cookies via our cookie consent banner upon arrival.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain commercial inquiry data only as long as necessary to fulfill the operational business purpose for which it was collected. We implement rigorous technical and organizational measures to safeguard against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you hold legal rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • The right to request rectification of inaccurate information.
  • The right to request immediate deletion of your data from our correspondence records.
